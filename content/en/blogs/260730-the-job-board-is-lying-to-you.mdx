## The Data Funnel

I spent two years working for myself. When I finally opened LinkedIn to test the waters, I searched for my skills and filtered by the past week.

The results were garbage. Hundreds of links pointed to companies I had never heard of. [Mercor](https://www.mercor.com "mention | AI talent platform that grew revenue from $1M to $500M in 17 months"). [micro1](https://www.micro1.ai "mention | AI talent platform that screens 1000+ per role and monitors audio/video/browser during interviews"). [Outlier](https://outlier.ai "mention | Scale AI's annotation platform with 700K contractors"). [DataAnnotation](https://dataannotation.tech "mention | Annotation platform whose parent Surge AI faces class action and holds a BBB D- rating").

They all wanted an "AI Trainer" or an "RLHF Evaluator." No salary bands. No team details. Just vague copy about the future of artificial intelligence. I kept scrolling. The main feed was worse. Finding an actual open position felt like digging through a landfill.

Did these companies ever intend to hire anyone? I doubt it.

## The Play

The business model is beautifully cynical.

They post a broad job listing. They make it sound revolutionary. Applicants swarm the posting. Within hours, candidates receive a "skills assessment" that takes three hours to complete. You finish the test. The company promises to be in touch. You never hear from them again.

<Callout emoji="🎣">
  This is a data acquisition pipeline. Your test work and interview recordings become free training data. [Mercor's TOS](https://www.mercor.com "mention | TOS grants commercial rights to applicant contributions") writes it right into the terms — they own commercial rights to applicant contributions. [micro1](https://www.micro1.ai "mention | Monitors audio, video, and browser tabs during interviews") tracks your audio, video, and browser tabs during interviews.
</Callout>

These are data brokers wearing the skin of recruitment firms. [Mercor](https://www.mercor.com "mention | Raised $100M from Thiel and Dorsey, conducted 100K+ interviews, does not disclose hires") raised **$100 million** from Peter Thiel. They ran **100,000 interviews** and spiked their revenue to **$500 million in 17 months**. Nobody knows how many people they actually hired. Meanwhile, [AI job postings are up 134%](https://www.hiringlab.org "reference | Indeed's Hiring Lab tracking AI posting growth vs tech decline") while real tech hiring is down **34%**.

## The Disguise

You learn to ignore the obvious spam. The real danger is the disguise.

I applied to a posting that looked completely normal. Traditional title. Real company. Valid salary range. I applied. I got an interview.

Nobody showed up. An AI interviewed me. I recorded responses into a camera, answering prompts alone. Then came a one-hour test. An AI scored it. The result email was sent by an AI.

No human was involved at any point. Not in screening. Not in interviewing. Not in grading. Not in communicating the result.

<Callout emoji="🎭">
  The posting looked like a real job. The interview was a recorded video session. The test was annotation work. The entire process existed to extract an hour of my time — and whatever data my face, voice, and answers generated — without a single human on the other end.
</Callout>

This is the pattern. A listing that looks legitimate. An interview with no interviewer. A test scored by software. A rejection email written by a language model. You never talk to a person. You never get feedback. You just give them data.

If a coding job asks you to grade chatbot text, walk away. If you find yourself talking into a camera for a one-way interview, you are generating training data. If the entire hiring process has zero humans in it, the process is not hiring. It is harvesting.

## Ghost Jobs and the Doom Loop

Then there are the ghost jobs. Openings that simply do not exist.

Zero funding. Zero headcount. Companies post them to harvest resumes or project fake growth. A [Columbia Law Review](https://www.columbialawreview.org/content/ghost-jobs/ "reference | Legal analysis finding 48% of tech job listings are phantom postings") study found **48% of tech job listings** are complete phantoms. [Clarify Capital](https://www.clarifycapital.com "reference | Survey finding 1 in 3 employers post jobs with no intent to hire") found **1 in 3 employers** admit posting jobs with no intent to fill them. **68% of companies** believe ghost jobs have positive revenue impact. Employers do this intentionally because it suppresses wages and builds pipelines.

[LinkedIn's overall ghost rate is 27%](https://resumeup.ai "reference | Q2 2026 analysis of ghost job rates by platform"). More than a quarter of every listing on the platform does not exist.

The hiring market is trapped in a doom loop. [Greenhouse](https://www.greenhouse.com "mention | ATS platform whose CEO described the AI hiring doom loop") CEO Daniel Chait describes the cycle: AI tools let applicants spam hundreds of jobs a day — **254 applicants per posting, up 412%**. Companies panic and deploy AI screening to auto-reject people. **75% of companies let AI reject candidates without human oversight.** Candidates fight back by hiding [prompt injections](https://www.shrm.org "reference | SHRM reporting 41% of job seekers use prompt injections in resumes") in their resumes. **91% of recruiters** caught candidate fraud in 2025. Trust is completely dead — **70% of hiring managers trust AI, only 8% of job seekers call it fair.** The humans on both sides lost control of the machine.

## The Reality

Do not wait for the government to save you. [Ontario passed a law](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/00e41 "reference | Ontario's Working for Workers Five Act, effective Jan 2026, requiring AI disclosure and vacancy status") — Bill 190, effective January 2026, the strongest in North America. [New York's S8877](https://www.nysenate.gov "reference | NY state bill requiring employers to disclose if postings represent active vacancies, passed Senate April 2026") passed the state Senate in April 2026. [The Texas Attorney General](https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov "reference | Texas AG investigating LinkedIn for profiting from fake job listings while selling Premium subscriptions") is investigating LinkedIn for profiting off fake listings while selling Premium subscriptions. LinkedIn responded by adding a verified badge. **Half the jobs on the platform still lack it.**

No US state has enacted enforceable law as of July 2026.

I stopped trusting the board. I read the TOS before clicking apply. I refuse unpaid assessments that take more than thirty minutes. I search the company name on [the BBB](https://www.bbb.org "reference | Better Business Bureau where Surge AI holds a D- rating"), [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com "reference | Employee reviews that reveal Scale AI's 2.4/5 rating"), and [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/recruitinghell/ "reference | Community documenting hiring abuses and ghost jobs") before investing time.

Treat your application data like a commodity. That is exactly how the system views it. The job board wants volume. The AI companies want your free labor. The corporate HR departments want to manipulate the market. The machine is functioning flawlessly. You are just the fuel.
